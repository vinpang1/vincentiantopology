# English · Quick (view)

Length: **300–500** · standalone · no S1–S4

Core → [`../_base.md`](../_base.md) · Citations → [`../citations.md`](../citations.md) §英文

## Reader psychology

- 3 seconds: relevant to me?
- One takeaway; low cognitive tax

## Four moves

1. **Hook** — concrete scene or inner line
2. **Name** — label the feeling (not the verdict)
3. **Takeaway** — "If you remember one thing: …"
4. **Close** — light Open; no forced sequel

## Citations

- **0–1** familiar named example in body; Western-primary
- ~4 rows: `Author | Work | Passage`

## Full English

Title, headings, body, citations, Open — all English.

## Avoid

Five-move premium path on quick pieces; jargon pile; multiple theses; UUID / database terms in body
